# BUSINESS_MODEL.md
### How FabVerify Makes Money and Survives
> The honest economics. Built on real market research including why Zilingo ($308M raised) and ReshaMandi (India's largest) died. The core lesson drives everything: multiple revenue streams, enterprise-anchored, asset-light, AI-built, grow profitably.

---

## THE MARKET (real, not hype)
- India textile & apparel: projected **$350B by 2030**, ~10% CAGR; India is the 3rd-largest textile exporter.
- Problem validated by funded competitors: Fabriclore, Locofast, Fashinza, Groyyo, ReshaMandi, Zilingo. Investors (Tiger Global etc.) put real money in "digitize the fragmented textile supply chain."

## THE GRAVEYARD (why most died — and how we avoid it)
- **Zilingo** raised $308M, died 2022: never profitable, B2C-cash-burn core, fraud/mismanagement.
- **ReshaMandi** (India's largest) collapsed.
- Root causes across failed B2B marketplaces: **thin margins + heavy human-ops that scales badly + inventory risk + burning VC to chase vanity GMV.**

### How FabVerify structurally avoids all three killers
1. **No inventory** — asset-light, no buyback risk. (killed Zilingo/ReshaMandi)
2. **AI-built** — Claude Code replaces the hundreds of ops people those companies hired; a fraction of the burn.
3. **Enterprise-anchored, multi-stream** — not take-rate-only; can grow **profitably and slowly**, never burn to survive.

---

## REVENUE STREAMS (never take-rate alone — B1)
1. **Transaction take rate** — 3–5% on orders through escrow.
2. **Verification fees** — Silver ₹999, Gold ₹4,999 (one-time).
3. **FabTalent commission** — 10–15% on freelancer gigs.
4. **Credit spread** — thin honest margin on FabPay Later / FabFloat / FabMaterial (via RBI-registered NBFC partner).
5. **Enterprise SaaS** — monthly subscription. **THE PROFIT ENGINE.** One ₹8L/month enterprise client > 200 small transactions.
6. **Data / FabPrice / FabStandard** — later.

**Rule:** take-rate is thin and ops-heavy; enterprise SaaS is what makes us profitable while transactions scale. Prioritize enterprise revenue.

## MONTH-BY-MONTH ECONOMICS (conservative, honest)

### Phase 1 — Months 1–6 (pilot, near-zero revenue)
- Users: 20–50 manufacturers, 10–30 brands (from founder network).
- Monthly GMV: ₹5–20L. Revenue: ₹30k–₹1.1L/mo (take rate + verification). Cost: ₹15–40k/mo.
- **Net: ~break-even.** Honest truth: you make almost nothing. You're proving trust. Normal and correct.

### Phase 2 — Months 7–18 (traction, first real profit)
- Fabindia door opens; first enterprise pilot; network word-of-mouth.
- Users: 200–500 manufacturers, 100–300 brands, 1–3 enterprise pilots. GMV: ₹1–3 crore/mo.
- Revenue: ₹8–30L/mo (take rate ₹4–12L + verification ₹1–3L + FabTalent ₹0.5–2L + **enterprise SaaS ₹2–15L**). Cost: ₹5–15L/mo (team 3–5 + infra).
- **Net profit: ₹2–15L/month.** Enterprise becomes the engine; the Fabindia relationship matters most here.

### Phase 3 — Months 19–36 (scale, real business)
- Users: 2,000–5,000 businesses, 10–30 enterprise clients. GMV: ₹10–40 crore/mo.
- Revenue: ₹1–3.4 crore/mo (take rate ₹40L–1.6cr + **enterprise SaaS ₹40L–1.2cr** + credit/FabTalent/verification ₹20–60L). Cost: ₹40L–1.5cr/mo.
- **Net profit: ₹40L–2 crore/month.**

## THE HONEST CAVEATS
1. **Trust takes time** — year-1 revenue trends to the LOW end of ranges; escrow adoption is slow. Don't expect middle numbers early.
2. **Take-rate alone is a trap** — enterprise SaaS is survival.
3. **Escrow/credit need partners + licenses** — months to set up; credit-spread revenue comes later.
4. **Cash-flow discipline is everything** — the killer is spending ahead of revenue. Stay lean, let AI do the work, don't hire 500, don't chase vanity GMV.

---

## THE VERDICT
- **Does it make sense?** Yes — real $350B market, validated problem, and a model that structurally avoids what killed the others.
- **Does it make money month-by-month?** Not meaningfully for ~6 months. Modest (₹2–15L/mo) months 7–18 IF the Fabindia enterprise pilot lands. Real (₹40L–2cr/mo) months 19–36 IF lean + enterprise SaaS grows.
- **Biggest risk:** discipline, not the idea. Burn cash → join the graveyard. Stay lean + enterprise-anchored → become the rare profitable one.
- **Biggest asset:** the Fabindia relationship — shortcuts the slowest, most expensive part (first high-value enterprise client).

---

## PRICING (initial, adjustable)
- Verification: Silver ₹999, Gold ₹4,999 (one-time).
- Take rate: 3–5% (lower for high-volume/enterprise-linked vendors).
- FabTalent: 10–15% commission.
- Enterprise SaaS: tiered — Foundational ~₹2L/mo, Growth ~₹4L/mo, Enterprise ~₹8L/mo (indicative; contract-negotiated). Tier upgrades pre-agreed with 30-day notice.
- Credit: thin honest spread over partner NBFC cost; APR fully disclosed; no hidden charges; no prepayment penalty.
