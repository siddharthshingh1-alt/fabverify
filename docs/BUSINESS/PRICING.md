# BUSINESS: PRICING
## VERIFICATION
Silver ₹999, Gold ₹4,999 (one-time). Bronze free (accessibility — never lock out the small player).

## TRANSACTION
3–5% take rate; lower for high-volume/enterprise-linked vendors.

## FABTALENT
10–15% commission on gigs.

## ENTERPRISE SaaS (indicative, contract-negotiated)
Foundational ~₹2L/mo · Growth ~₹4L/mo · Enterprise ~₹8L/mo. Tier upgrades pre-agreed, 30-day notice.

## CREDIT
Thin honest spread over partner NBFC cost. Full APR disclosed; KFS; no hidden charges; no prepayment penalty.

## PRINCIPLE
Fair by design — pricing never exploits the weaker party. Transparent both ways.
