# BUSINESS: KPIS
## NORTH-STAR
Real GMV flowing through escrow (verified, protected transactions) — proof the trust model works.

## TRUST
Verified users (by tier), verified-milestone on-time rate, dispute rate (target near-zero via QR), FabScore distribution.

## REVENUE
Enterprise SaaS MRR (the engine), take-rate revenue, verification revenue, FabTalent commission, credit spread.

## MISSION
Artisan direct-earning uplift ("₹X earned directly, ₹Y saved from middleman"), % of makers paid within 48hr (FabFloat), small-brand survival/reorder rate.

## GROWTH & HEALTH
Active users by type, retention/reorder rate, enterprise clients + net revenue retention, burn vs revenue (stay lean — discipline is the #1 risk).

## RULE
Never optimize vanity GMV at the expense of profitability (that killed Zilingo/ReshaMandi). Profitable growth > vanity growth.
