# MODULE: FABCREDIT
> Honest-by-design credit — the mission's hardest test.

## PURPOSE
Give artisans, small makers, and brands fair credit that never traps them — the opposite of hidden-charge NBFC lending.

## WHAT IT DOES
- FabPay Later (buyer pays 30/60/90; maker paid immediately).
- FabFloat (maker/artisan paid in 48hr on verified proof).
- FabMaterial (raw-material credit for artisans/makers).
- Key Fact Statement before accepting; APR fully disclosed.

## STATUS
All 🔴 (design-locked).

## KEY RULES (honest by design — M3, M4)
One all-in APR (no fake low rate). Plain-language KFS (local language + FabVoice). Every charge itemized; if not in KFS, cannot be charged. No prepayment penalty (RBI 2026). Humane recovery (8am–7pm, no harassment). Only RBI-registered partners (registration shown). FabScore honestly lowers cost. FabVerify is NOT the lender — partners are.

## CONNECTS TO
Identity/FabScore (risk → APR) · Payments/Escrow (FabFloat settlement) · Orders (pay-later timing) · Artisan/Brand/Maker dashboards.

## DATA
Planned credit_applications, credit_kfs.
