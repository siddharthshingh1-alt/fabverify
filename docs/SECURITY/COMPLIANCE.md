# COMPLIANCE.md
> The laws and standards FabVerify must satisfy.

## PAYMENTS (RBI / PSSA)
- FabVerify never holds customer money (M1). Escrow via licensed payment aggregator in a scheduled-bank escrow/nodal account.
- Permitted flows only; daily reconciliation; data localised in India.
- Merchant KYC gates payouts.

## CREDIT (RBI 2025–26)
- Key Fact Statement mandatory (APR + all charges + recovery process).
- No prepayment penalty on floating-rate loans to individuals/MSEs ≤₹50L (from 1 Jan 2026).
- Penal charges not a revenue tool; disclosed upfront.
- Recovery only 8am–7pm; no harassment.
- Only RBI-registered NBFC partners; registration shown.

## VERIFICATION / KYC
- Consent-based; Aadhaar via DigiLocker (never store the number); GST/Udyam/PAN/CIN checks.

## EU (moat / future)
- Digital Product Passport, due-diligence/traceability directives → FabVerify's QR chain generates the proof (Gold tier).
- Chemical compliance (REACH/AZO) records for trims/fabric.

## LABOR / ETHICAL
- Fair-pay ethos (artisan share largest); humane credit recovery; the mission encodes ethical treatment.
