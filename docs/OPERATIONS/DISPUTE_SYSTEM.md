# OPERATIONS: DISPUTE_SYSTEM
> Resolving disagreements with evidence.

## PRINCIPLE
QR + geo-tagged photo proof should make most disputes evidence-resolvable (near-zero dispute goal).

## FLOW
Party raises dispute → system surfaces the verified chain (scans, photos, timestamps, reconciliation) → FabVerify team reviews → resolution → escrow released/refunded/held accordingly.

## RULES
Payment held during genuine dispute (permitted flow). Evidence-first: the QR chain is the referee. Humane, fair, both sides heard. Anomaly-triggered holds only for genuinely impossible discrepancies (V5).

## STATUS
🔴 (design-locked). Depends on QR chain (Phase B).

## OPS
Dispute queue in Admin; SLA for resolution; pattern analysis (repeat offenders lose FabScore/verification).
