# PRODUCT: MVP_SCOPE
> The minimum that makes FabVerify real and trustworthy — not just a demo.

## MVP = what makes the trust promise real
Already live: signup/login, manufacturer profiles, discovery, enquiries, orders, messages, sample briefs, verification status.

## MVP-COMPLETING (Phase A — must-have to be usable by a real business)
- Real escrow (partner) — without money movement, nothing is real.
- Photos in Storage (not base64).
- WhatsApp notifications (users return).
- Admin verification approval (Silver/Gold complete).
- Order completion + delivery address.
- Password login; real SMS.

## MVP EXPLICITLY EXCLUDES (later phases)
Full QR chain, SMV engine, FabScore algorithm, all mill/trim/artisan/job-worker/FabTalent specific features, full enterprise (11 departments), credit products, EU moat. These are the roadmap AFTER the core is trustworthy.

## PRINCIPLE
A trustworthy small core beats a broad fake one. Make what exists real before adding breadth (ROADMAP Phase A).
