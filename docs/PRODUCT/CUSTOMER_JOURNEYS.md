# PRODUCT: CUSTOMER_JOURNEYS
> The emotional arc, not just the clicks — what each user feels and needs at each stage.

## PRIYA (first-time brand) — from fear to confidence
Discovery: overwhelmed, afraid of scams → sees verified makers + fair prices → relief.
Sampling: doesn't know she must sample → guided, won't let her skip → protected.
Order: terrified to pay a stranger → escrow holds money → safe.
Production: anxious, would spam WhatsApp → QR milestones + tracking → calm.
After: cash crunch looms → pay-later + forecast → survives. Reorders what sells → grows.

## RAJESH (manufacturer) — from powerless to trusted
Gets order via verified buyer → produces, scans milestones → paid in 48hr on proof → dignity.
Builds FabScore → cheaper credit than banks → grows. No more chasing payment.

## MEERA (artisan) — from exploited to seen
Direct order, fair price shown → materials on credit → crafts with honest timeline → paid directly, fast → sees "a middleman would've taken ₹Y." Feels seen and valued for the first time.

## VIKRAM (CEO) — from blind to in-control
Opens FabVerify → money/profit at a glance → drills into any department → sees every employee's work → no meeting needed. Runs the whole business in one place.

## RULE
Design each stage to move the user toward the positive emotion. The QR chain, escrow, and honest pricing exist to turn fear into trust.
