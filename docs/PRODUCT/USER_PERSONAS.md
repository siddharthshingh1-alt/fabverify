# PRODUCT: USER_PERSONAS
> Real humans behind the user types. Written to keep the build empathetic and concrete.

## MEERA — the home-based artisan
Chikankari karigar, Lucknow. 30 years' skill. Earns ₹200–500/day via middlemen who take the largest cut. Works from home between household duties on a ₹6,000 phone. Can't write English, take pro photos, or take online orders. NEEDS: direct fair pay, materials on credit, FabVerify to be her professional face, Hindi voice UI, dignity. The soul of the mission.

## RAJESH — the small manufacturer
Runs a 30-machine unit, Tirupur. Family business. Gets ghosted by buyers; chases payment for weeks; no leverage. NEEDS: verified buyers, escrow so he's paid on proof, FabFloat 48hr pay, FabScore that earns cheaper credit than banks, capacity calendar to book work.

## PRIYA — the first-time D2C founder
Started a clothing brand from a Tier-2 city. No idea what a fair price is; terrified of being scammed; risks over-ordering into dead stock. 90% like her fail (cash flow + dead stock). NEEDS: guided journey, honest costing, low-MOQ makers, escrow protection, pay-later, cash-flow forecast, easy reorder.

## VIKRAM — the enterprise CEO
Runs a ₹2,000cr brand. Sits in endless meetings to understand his own business. Vendors, orders, money scattered across systems. NEEDS: one place to run everything (fabric→accounts), money-first glance view, see every employee's work without a meeting, onboard existing vendors, restructure the org his way.

## SUNIL — the freelance QC inspector
Certified AQL + 4-Point. Hired per-job by brands and enterprises. NEEDS: profile + verified badge, portfolio that builds from gigs, digital inspection tools (AQL/4-Point calculators), geo-tagged proof he was at the factory, escrow payment.

## ANITA — the freelance merchandiser
Bridges buyers and manufacturers; lives in the T&A calendar; chases approvals all day. Hired per-order by small brands who can't afford ₹8–15L/yr. NEEDS: living T&A, approval-chasing tracker, multi-order health, costing engine, delegated (approval-gated) access to clients' workspaces.

## RULE
When building a feature, ask: does it make Meera/Rajesh/Priya stronger? If it quietly favors the powerful over them, redesign (Principle 1).
