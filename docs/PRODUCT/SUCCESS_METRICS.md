# PRODUCT: SUCCESS_METRICS
> How we know FabVerify is working. Business KPIs in docs/BUSINESS/KPIS.md.

## NORTH-STAR
Real GMV through escrow — verified, protected transactions.

## TRUST METRICS
- Verified users by tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold).
- Verified-milestone on-time rate.
- Dispute rate (target: near-zero via QR evidence).
- % makers paid within 48hr (FabFloat).

## MISSION METRICS
- Artisan direct-earning uplift (₹ earned directly; ₹ saved from middleman).
- Small-brand survival/reorder rate.
- Craft continuation (artisans staying active).

## PRODUCT-HEALTH METRICS
- Activation: signup → first order.
- Retention: reorder rate; monthly active by type.
- Feature adoption: verification uptake, escrow uptake, QR adoption by tier.

## ENTERPRISE METRICS
- Enterprise clients; SaaS MRR; net revenue retention; departments/seats used.
- CEO glance-view usage (do they run the business here?).

## THE FIVE SUCCESS SIGNALS (from VISION)
1. Artisan earns largest share, paid directly, sees middleman savings.
2. First-time founder places a fair, protected first order.
3. Manufacturer paid in 48hr on proof; FabScore earns cheap credit.
4. Enterprise runs sourcing→accounts in one place; CEO sees all at a glance.
5. European brand scans QR → full verified journey.
When these are true, FabVerify is the operating system of India's garment industry.
