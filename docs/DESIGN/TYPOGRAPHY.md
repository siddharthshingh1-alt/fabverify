# DESIGN: TYPOGRAPHY
## FONTS
- Headings: Montserrat (confident, modern).
- Body: Inter (highly legible).

## SCALE (guidance)
Clear hierarchy: page title > section > card title > body > caption. Generous line-height for readability, especially for low-literacy/older users.

## RULES
- Legibility over style — many users are on cheap phones in bright light.
- Support Hindi/local scripts (FabVoice + multilingual). Don't assume Latin-only.
- Numbers (prices, meters, pieces, order IDs) must be unambiguous and easy to scan.
