# DESIGN: DESIGN_SYSTEM
> The visual system of FabVerify. Centralized in app/theme.ts (sweep hardcoded colors — X4).

## THE FEEL
Calm, clear, dignified, trustworthy. Speaks to people often disrespected by systems — never patronizing, especially to artisans and small makers (Principle 12). Serious fintech-grade trust, warm human mission.

## TOKENS (locked)
Background #07122a · Cards #0D1B33 · Border #1C3050 · Gold accent #f2ca50 · Text primary #E2E8F0 · Text secondary #7A8FA8 · Danger #e34948.
Headings: Montserrat. Body: Inter.

## PRINCIPLES
- Minimal, purposeful; no clutter.
- Dashboards show attention-first content, not nav-duplicating cards (P6, P12).
- Mobile-first for FabChat + factory-floor tools; desktop-first for the main platform + enterprise.
- Photo-forward for inventory (find by looking).
- Local language + voice support in artisan/factory tools.
